Everyone feels bad sometimes. But if the heavy feeling has stayed for a long time, or grown so big that everyday life is hard, you deserve support.

## You might feel

- Worry that won't let go, or a racing heart without knowing why.
- That you can't face things you used to enjoy.
- That you sleep too much or too little.
- That you want to be left alone all the time – or can't stand being alone.

## You are not weak

Feeling bad isn't something you chose, and it's nothing to be ashamed of. It's your body and mind saying something needs to change.

## What can help

- **Say it out loud to someone.** Sharing the heavy stuff often makes it a little easier to carry.
- **Small steps count.** Shower, go outside for ten minutes, eat something. That's not little – it's a lot.
- **Get care if it doesn't pass.** Youth clinics and health centres are there for you. You can also call 1177.

<Callout>
Are you thinking about taking your life? You are not alone, and help exists right now. Call **90101** (the Suicide Line) or **112** in an emergency.
</Callout>
