Home should be a place where you can rest. But for many people it isn't. It might be arguments, a parent who drinks too much, mental illness in the family, money worries or rules that feel impossible to live with.

## You might feel

- Tense when it's time to go home.
- Responsible for things adults should be handling.
- Ashamed, and you don't want anyone to know.
- Angry, sad or completely empty. Sometimes all at once.

Every feeling is okay. None of them mean something is wrong with you.

## It's not your fault

How adults behave is never your responsibility. You can't fix your family. And you don't have to cope with everything alone.

## What can help

- **Talk to someone you trust.** A friend, a teacher, the school counsellor – or us.
- **Find a safe place.** Somewhere you can breathe. The library, a youth centre, a friend's kitchen.
- **Write down how things are.** It's often easier to find words once they're on paper.

<Callout>
You have the right to be safe. If an adult hurts you, or you don't dare go home, social services can help. We can support you in reaching out – you don't have to do it alone.
</Callout>
